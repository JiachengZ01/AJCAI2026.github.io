AJCAI 2026 Responsible AI Forum

When is an AI system ready to earn our trust?

What should research result change about the AI system a team builds? And what happens in practice that should change the next research question? Imagine a corporate testing an AI assistant. It answers routine questions accurately yet gives a customer the wrong advice when they ask for support. What would the testing have needed to show, who would spot the mistake, and how could the customer get it put right? This forum brings together two machine learning researchers and a banking technology leader to explore both directions.

Join researchers from University of Waikato and Adelaide University and technology leader from the Bank of New Zealand to explore this hypothetical case and the choices it raises for anyone deploying AI. The forum is for researchers, practitioners and engineers who build, evaluate or oversee AI systems. Bring a question from your own work for the audience discussion.

Invited panellists

Professor Bernhard Pfahringer

Professor of Computer Science and Co-Director of the AI Institute, University of Waikato

Bernhard is a Professor of Computer Science and Co-Director of the AI Institute at the University of Waikato. His research spans machine learning and data mining, with a particular interest in how models learn from streams of data and adapt as conditions change.

Mr Lee Challoner-Miles

General Manager, Digital, Data and AI, Bank of New Zealand

Lee leads Digital, Data and AI for BNZ Technology, bringing extensive experience across customer-facing technology, digital channels, data platforms and enterprise transformation. His background spans banking, telecommunications, programme delivery and self-service technology across multiple markets. Lee brings a strong technology and leadership perspective to the panel and will be looking for AI solutions that are bold, well-designed and capable of improving the way customers and teams experience banking.

Dr Yiliao (Lia) Song

Lecturer in Artificial Intelligence, Adelaide University

Lia is a lecturer (teaching-research balanced) at Adelaide University and a researcher at the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML) and the Australian Research Centre for Interactive and Virtual Environments (IVE). In the same time, she is a Visiting Scholar at the Australian Artificial Intelligence Institute (AAII) at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS). Her research focuses on trustworthy machine learning and reliable AI systems. As an educator, she specialises in artificial intelligence and machine learning, with interests extending beyond teaching technical foundations to investigating how emerging AI tools can improve teaching and learning in higher education. She is also involved in developing and evaluating the Chatmate generative AI platform.
