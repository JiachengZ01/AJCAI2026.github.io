AJCAI 2026 Industry Day

Where AI research meets industry

Join us on Sunday 29 November 2026 for the AJCAI 2026 Industry Day, held as part of the Australasian Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence at the University of Auckland. The day brings together AI researchers and industry leaders from across Australia and New Zealand to share how artificial intelligence is being built, deployed and scaled in the real world.

Hear from founders, CEOs and technical leaders working on AI for customer feedback analytics, hydraulic engineering, computational chemistry, demand forecasting and more. The program features a keynote from our Platinum Sponsor Maincode, four industry talks, a panel discussion on the challenges and opportunities at the boundary of research and industry, and plenty of time to network with speakers and conference delegates.

10:40–11:30 · Platinum Sponsor Keynote

From Data Centres to Intelligence: Building Australia's Sovereign AI Future

David Lemphers and Yue Yang · Maincode
Chair: Yi Mei

Abstract

The future of artificial intelligence will not be defined by model scale alone. It will also depend on who can build the infrastructure, develop the models, and turn cutting-edge research into deployable intelligence. As AI becomes increasingly central to science, industry, and national capability, a critical question emerges: What does it really take to build an AI ecosystem from the ground up?

In this keynote, we share Maincode's journey towards building sovereign AI capabilities in Australia, spanning the physical infrastructure powering modern AI systems to the research and engineering behind their models. Mr. Dave Lemphers will explore the foundations of AI infrastructure, drawing on Maincode's experience with Australian-hosted data centres and high-performance GPU clusters. He will discuss the engineering realities of hosting and operating AI compute, and how infrastructure choices shape the scalability, efficiency, and accessibility of AI development. Dr Yue Yang will take the audience inside Maincode's model development and training research. Through two examples—Matilda Platypus, which explores creating language models at flexible sizes through based on existing models, and Matilda Jev, a specialised model designed for efficient, low-latency decision-making. Together, the talk offers a perspective on building AI beyond the model itself, highlighting the interplay between computing infrastructure, research innovation, and real-world deployment—and the opportunities for Australia to shape its own AI future.

David Lemphers

Dave Lemphers is the Co-founder and CEO of Maincode, an Australian AI company dedicated to building sovereign AI capabilities, from computing infrastructure to foundation models. With over 20 years of experience in technology, AI, and large-scale distributed systems, Dave has held senior technical and leadership positions at Microsoft, Amazon Web Services (AWS), and Easygo. A serial entrepreneur and inventor, he has co-founded and successfully exited multiple technology startups and holds several US patents in machine learning and AI. At Maincode, Dave leads the development of Australian-owned AI infrastructure, including high-performance GPU clusters, alongside the company's foundation models and AI platforms. His work focuses on bridging advanced AI research, infrastructure engineering, and real-world deployment.

Dr Yue Yang

Dr Yue Yang is an AI Research Scientist at Maincode. Her research focuses on large language models, reinforcement learning, efficient model training, and trustworthy AI. At Maincode, she plays a key role in foundation model development and post-training research, contributing to projects including MainCoder and the Matilda family of models, such as Matilda Chat, Matilda Code, and Matilda Jev. Her research has been published at leading international AI conferences, including ICML, ICLR, NeurIPS, ACL, EMNLP, and AAMAS. Her work InterIDEAS received the Best Special Theme Paper Award at EMNLP 2025, and she was also recognised with the Best Industry Paper Award at the 2025 Kingston Conference. Bridging academic research and industrial innovation, she is dedicated to advancing efficient, reliable, and deployable AI systems that translate cutting-edge research into real-world impact.

14:00–14:30 · Industry Talk 2

Computer Vision on the Shop Floor: Lessons from Deploying Real-Time Quality Control in Hydraulic Hose Assembly

Chair: Yuqian Lu

Zexuan (Martin) Zhu

R&D Team Leader, BOA Hydraulics | LinkedIn

Dr Zexuan Zhu is the R&D Team Lead for Software & Hardware Innovation at BOA Hydraulics, a New Zealand hydraulics company, where he leads a team spanning product management, backend development and systems integration. His work brings AI and computer vision into day-to-day industrial operations. This includes BOA’s camera-based quality control system for hydraulic hose assembly, which gives operators real-time instructions and alerts on the production floor. Zexuan holds a PhD in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Auckland, where he developed an augmented-reality-based intelligent machine window for machine tools, with computer vision at its core. He is also an AWS Certified AI Business Strategist.
