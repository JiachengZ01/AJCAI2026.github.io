About the Program

AJCAI 2026 is pleased to introduce the New Faculty Highlights program, which recognises emerging researchers who have recently begun independent academic or research careers in artificial intelligence within Australasia and the wider Oceania region in the south-west Pacific, including Australia, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea, and other Pacific Island countries and territories. Six applicants are expected to be selected.

Selected applicants will be featured on the AJCAI 2026 conference website and will be invited to deliver an in-person highlight talk at AJCAI 2026 in Auckland, New Zealand. The program provides a forum for new faculty members to share their research vision, key contributions and future directions, and to build connections and collaborations across institutions in the region.

Eligibility

Applicants must meet all of the following requirements:

  • PhD completion: the applicant completed their PhD on or after 1 January 2019. For the purpose of eligibility, PhD completion means the date of receipt of formal confirmation from the granting institution that all requirements for the degree had been met.
  • Career stage: the applicant started, or is due to start, their first faculty appointment at Academic Level B or C—or an equivalent independent research appointment—between 1 January 2024 and 27 November 2026.
  • Regional appointment: the applicant currently holds, or has accepted an appointment commencing no later than 27 November 2026, at a university or research organisation in Australasia or the wider Oceania region in the south-west Pacific.

Applicants whose eligibility has been affected by career interruptions or non-traditional career paths are encouraged to contact the Program Coordinator before applying.

New Faculty Highlight Talk

Selected applicants will be invited to give an in-person presentation of approximately 20–30 minutes at AJCAI 2026 (including Q&A). The final presentation length, format and schedule will be confirmed in the notification of outcome.

The talk should present a broad overview of the speaker's research program rather than focus on a single paper. Speakers are encouraged to address:

  • their overall research vision and directions;
  • selected contributions and their significance;
  • connections across their research to date; and
  • promising directions for future research.
Attendance and costs

Selected applicants are expected to attend AJCAI 2026 in person and are responsible for their own registration, travel and accommodation costs. At least a valid day registration covering the day of their scheduled presentation is required (NZD 350). Prospective applicants with limited funding are encouraged to contact the Program Coordinator before applying.

Application Materials

Eligible researchers should submit the following materials through the online application form:

  • Curriculum vitae: in the applicant's preferred format.
  • New Faculty Highlight Statement: up to two pages, excluding references, using the Springer conference proceedings format. The statement should describe the proposed talk, including the applicant's research vision, key contributions, connections across their work and promising future directions.
  • Reference letter: one reference letter from a referee.

Selection

Applications will be considered on the basis of the applicant's research vision, the significance and coherence of their contributions, the promise of their future research program, and the potential value of the talk to the broad AJCAI community. The Selection Committee may also consider disciplinary, institutional and regional diversity when shaping the program.
