Encore Track

Friday, 27 November 2026 · Case Room 2 (260-057) · Auckland time (NZDT)

To highlight impactful and cutting-edge research in machine learning and artificial intelligence, we are pleased to announce the Encore Track at AJCAI 2026. This track is designed to showcase significant research works that have already appeared at other prestigious venues, while providing opportunities for further interaction, discussion, and knowledge exchange among researchers.

The Encore Track welcomes both Journal-to-Conference and Conference-to-Conference submissions:

  • Journal-to-Conference Encore: high-quality research recently published, in press, or accepted by leading journals, presented to the AJCAI community in a conference setting.
  • Conference-to-Conference Encore: high-quality research recently published or accepted at leading conferences, providing an opportunity to engage with a new audience and foster further discussion and collaboration.

Researchers are invited to submit the title, abstract, and author information for eligible recent publications that have been published, are in press, or have been accepted since 1 June 2025. Suitable venues include, but are not limited to, top-tier conferences and journals such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ECCV, ICCV, CVPR, ACL, AAAI, JMLR, and IEEE TPAMI.

Presentation Format: Accepted submissions will be presented in the AJCAI 2026 Encore Track, which will take place during the conference week in Auckland, New Zealand, from 27–30 November 2026.

Review Process: The review process will begin immediately after submission. Acceptance will be based on the quality and relevance of the submitted work. To enrich discussions at the conference, we particularly welcome submissions that demonstrate novel insights while contributing to the diversity and breadth of the AJCAI 2026 program.
