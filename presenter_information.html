Preparing and loading your presentation

  • It is the presenter’s responsibility to ensure that all slides are in the correct order and built into the PowerPoint presentation.
  • Bring your presentation on a USB drive for easy transfer. An on-site student volunteer will be available to assist.
  • Each oral session breakout room has a designated lectern computer. Load your presentation in your allocated conference room during the breaks before your session block begins, on the day of your presentation.
  • If you wish to present from your own laptop, bring all relevant adaptors and cables.

Important: no presenter view

The venue lectern computers do not support presenter view. Please bring any notes you may need in printed form.

Session rooms

  • All session rooms will be set up in lecture style.
  • Every room includes a lectern fitted with a microphone.
  • Wireless internet is available throughout the conference venue.
  • You can use the in-room lectern computer for online elements, such as live polling, streaming videos or accessing web-based content. If you plan to play YouTube videos within your presentation, download and embed them in the presentation beforehand to avoid disruptions from advertisements and buffering.

Find your session and room in the conference programme.
